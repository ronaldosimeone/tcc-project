### Is it the same compressor (APU)?

## Reading

* `production` = the exact ONNX artefacts + card thresholds the backend serves (trained on MetroPT-3 + MetroPT 2022, which contain one oil leak).
* `no oil in training` = same nested recipe, trained only on data before the 2022 oil leak: models that never saw an oil failure. Comparing it with `production` isolates what ONE example of a failure type buys on the next occurrence.
* `physics twin` = grey-box residuals calibrated on MetroPT2's own healthy weeks (no labels, no failure examples).
* Lead vs LPS is the operational comparison: LPS is the alarm the train already has. A positive value is warning time the platform adds over the status quo.
* Two failures: every rate here is a count over n = 2 and must be read as such.
